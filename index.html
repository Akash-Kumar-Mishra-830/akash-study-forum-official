Our Operating Architecture

The H-G-B-T-A Pedagogical Framework

How our daily offline workflow systematically eliminates conceptual weaknesses.

H

Advanced Learning Technologies

(Hard Work) Complex science mechanisms and 3D vector geometry are taught using interactive visual models. We bridge the gap between abstract textbook formulas and intuitive physical understanding.

G

Premier Faculty Mentors

(Growth) Led by passionate educators who know each student's name, grasping speed, and weak topics. Mentors remain on campus post-lecture until the day's homework concepts are resolved.

B

Comprehensive E-Resources

(Basic Concepts) Curated printed theory sheets, formula handbooks, and graded question banks (Easy $\to$ Moderate $\to$ Advanced) integrated with digitized solution sets for structured self-study at home.

T

Rigorous Performance Tracking

(Time Management) Bi-weekly full-length tests conducted on standardized OMR sheets. Every paper produces an error breakdown identifying whether marks were lost to calculation slip-ups, time pressure, or conceptual gaps.

A

Individual Development Plan

(Achievement) Because no two students learn at the exact same pace, our coordinators tailor weekly micro-targets for struggling students to rebuild fundamentals before advancing to high-difficulty problems.

Privacy & Data

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Draft for ASF review: This notice describes the website's current data handling and ASF's stated retention targets. ASF should confirm its data-sharing practices, service providers, and applicable legal requirements before public launch.

Who handles your information

Akash Study Forum (ASF), Luhungpur, Kotwa Road, Lucknow – 226001, Uttar Pradesh, India, handles information submitted through this website. Contact ASF by phone or WhatsApp at +91 9935048088 for privacy questions or requests.

Information collected and why

  • Coaching enquiries: student and parent/guardian names, phone number, class, course, and any optional school, preferred call time, or demo details. ASF stores enquiries in its server database so staff can respond and manage admissions.
  • Paid Study Hub orders: student name, phone number, selected test, order/payment status, and submitted UPI transaction reference (UTR). Staff use these details to process the order, verify payment, and provide access. Access codes are stored as hashes.
  • CRM staff accounts: authorized staff names and email addresses, password hashes, account roles, and password-reset tokens are stored in the server database to provide restricted CRM access. Password reset links are sent through ASF's configured email provider; passwords are not sent by email.
  • AI tutor: the questions and conversation history you send are forwarded by the ASF server to the AI service configured for the site so it can generate a reply. ASF's application does not save these chats in its database. The AI provider may handle them under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not include passwords, phone numbers, or other sensitive personal details in a question.
  • Reviews and Study Hub content: reviews, study schedules, notes, and free-test information currently use this browser's local storage; they are not automatically submitted to ASF's server. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to see that saved information.
  • Basic technical data: the server uses the connecting IP address in process memory for request rate limits; it is not intentionally saved to the application database. The CRM and Study Hub staff portals use login session cookies; their authentication cookies are HTTP-only and restricted to the relevant staff API paths.

When information is shared

Enquiries and order records are available to staff who can access the ASF server and the relevant password-protected portal. CRM staff account emails are shared with ASF's configured email provider only when sending a requested password-reset link. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts. When you choose to open WhatsApp, YouTube, Google Maps, or a test link, that service receives the information involved in that interaction under its own policies. AI tutor questions are sent to the AI provider configured by ASF. ASF should verify its organizational data-sharing practices before adopting this draft as its final policy.

Storage and retention

ASF's stated retention targets are up to 12 months for coaching enquiries and up to 1 month for paid Study Hub order records. Staff handle deletion manually; the website does not automatically delete records when those periods pass. ASF has not yet specified when each period starts, so ask ASF using the contact details above about a particular record or to request correction or deletion. These targets do not override any applicable legal record-keeping requirements.

The server stores enquiries, orders, CRM staff accounts, and password-reset tokens in its SQLite database. Password-reset tokens expire after 30 minutes and are single-use. Authorized staff can manually delete an individual enquiry in the CRM or a paid order and its access code in the Study Hub portal. Database backups may retain a copy after a server record is deleted; backup files need to be rotated or securely removed separately.

Browser-local reviews and Study Hub content remain in that browser until they are cleared by the user or browser. When a staff member first logs in, existing browser-local CRM leads and videos are imported to the server; after a successful import, those two local data stores are removed from that browser.

Children and guardians

ASF serves school-age students. If a student is under 18, a parent or legal guardian should submit or confirm enquiries and paid-test orders. The website does not independently verify a person's age or guardian status. Please avoid including a child's sensitive personal information in public reviews or AI tutor questions.

Your choices and questions

You may contact ASF to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you provided. Clearing browser storage removes locally saved reviews and Study Hub content from that browser but does not delete server records or database backups. For server records, contact ASF directly.

Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when website features or data handling change. Contact ASF by phone or WhatsApp at +91 9935048088 with questions.
